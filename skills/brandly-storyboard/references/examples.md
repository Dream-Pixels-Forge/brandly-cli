## Example 1: 30-Second Perfume Commercial

**Style:** Cinematic | **Budget:** 200 credits | **Duration:** 30s

```markdown
# STORYBOARD: Lumière Perfume — "First Light"
# Style: cinematic | Total Duration: 30s

MASTER CONTEXT:
A woman discovers the essence of morning — the moment between sleep and waking
when the world feels new. The perfume Lumière captures this feeling in a bottle.
Warm golden tones, soft skin textures, intimate and aspirational.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SHOT 1 — ESTABLISHING (4s)
Context:   Dawn, quiet bedroom, city skyline through window
Subject:   Empty bed, rumpled sheets, perfume bottle on nightstand
Action:    Morning light slowly fills the room
Camera:    Slow push in toward the bottle
Prompt:    "Dawn light streaming through sheer curtains into a modern bedroom.
            A glass perfume bottle sits on a marble nightstand. Golden hour
            light catches condensation on the bottle. Camera slowly pushes in.
            Soft, dreamy atmosphere. Cinematic, Kodak Vision3, warm tones."
Assets:    --reference-images "location_modern_bedroom.png"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SHOT 2 — MEDIUM (3s)
Context:   Same bedroom, woman entering frame
Subject:   Woman, mid-20s, bare shoulders, tousled hair
Action:    She reaches for the bottle, sunlight on her skin
Camera:    Static medium shot, waist-level framing
Prompt:    "Woman in her late 20s with tousled blonde hair, bare shoulders,
            reaching toward a perfume bottle on a nightstand. Morning golden
            light on her skin. Soft, intimate mood. Shallow depth of field.
            Cinematic, 8K quality."
Assets:    --reference-images "character_maya.png,location_modern_bedroom.png"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SHOT 3 — CLOSE-UP (3s)
Context:   Focus on her hand and the bottle
Subject:   Fingers touching the rose gold cap
Action:    Slowly twisting the cap open
Camera:    Close-up, static
Prompt:    "Close-up of a woman's delicate fingers unscrewing the rose gold cap
            of a glass perfume bottle. Condensation droplets on the glass.
            Soft morning light. Shallow depth of field, bokeh background.
            Product photography, 8K."
Assets:    --reference-images "product_lumiere.png"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SHOT 4 — EXTREME CLOSE-UP (3s)
Context:   The spray moment
Subject:   Perfume mist catching light
Action:    First spray, mist suspended in air
Camera:    ECU, high frame rate feel
Prompt:    "Extreme close-up of perfume mist spraying from a glass bottle.
            Individual droplets catching golden light, suspended in air.
            Macro photography, cinematic lighting, 8K."
Assets:    --reference-images "product_lumiere.png"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SHOT 5 — CLOSE-UP (4s)
Context:   Her face, eyes closed, experiencing the scent
Subject:   Woman's face, serene expression
Action:    Eyes closing, slight smile, inhaling deeply
Camera:    Slow push in to her face
Prompt:    "Close-up of a woman's face, eyes closed, gently inhaling. Serene
            expression of pure contentment. Golden hour light on her skin.
            Soft focus, romantic atmosphere. Cinematic, film grain, 8K."
Assets:    --reference-images "character_maya.png"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SHOT 6 — ORBITAL (4s)
Context:   Hero product reveal
Subject:   Perfume bottle centered, woman blurred in background
Action:    Camera orbits 360° around the bottle
Camera:    Orbital, slow and smooth
Prompt:    "Orbital shot rotating 360 degrees around a luxury perfume bottle
            (Lumière). Bottle centered, sharp focus. Woman softly blurred
            in background. Golden light环绕. Clean, premium aesthetic.
            Product hero shot, 8K."
Assets:    --reference-images "product_lumiere.png,location_modern_bedroom.png"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SHOT 7 — WIDE (4s)
Context:   Woman standing at window, bottle in hand
Subject:   Full figure, silhouette against golden light
Action:    She looks out at the city, bottle held gracefully
Camera:    Wide shot, static
Prompt:    "Wide shot of a woman standing at a floor-to-ceiling window,
            golden hour light flooding the room. She holds the perfume bottle
            gracefully, looking out at the city skyline. Aspirational,
            empowering mood. Cinematic, warm color grade, 8K."
Assets:    --reference-images "character_maya.png,location_modern_bedroom.png"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SHOT 8 — HERO LOCKUP (5s)
Context:   Product + brand
Subject:   Perfume bottle, tagline area
Action:    Static hero shot, subtle light shimmer on bottle
Camera:    Static, clean commercial
Prompt:    "Clean hero shot of Lumière perfume bottle on a marble surface.
            Golden light reflecting off the glass. Minimal background.
            Space at top for text overlay. Commercial product photography,
            8K, studio quality."
Assets:    --reference-images "product_lumiere.png"

# END STORYBOARD — Total: 30s
```

## Example 2: 15-Second Social Ad (Sneakers)

**Style:** Commercial | **Budget:** 80 credits | **Duration:** 15s

```markdown
# STORYBOARD: UrbanStep Sneakers — "Ready"
# Style: commercial | Total Duration: 15s

MASTER CONTEXT:
High-energy urban sneaker ad. Young, confident, ready to move. The UrbanStep
pro sneakers in "MidnightVolt" colorway — black with electric green accents.
Target: Gen Z, fitness-conscious, streetwear culture.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SHOT 1 — LOW ANGLE (2s)
Context:   Urban alley, concrete, graffiti
Subject:   Sneaker sole hitting pavement
Action:    Foot lands, impact visible
Camera:    Low angle, static, sharp focus on shoe
Prompt:    "Low angle hero shot of a black sneaker with electric green sole
            landing on wet concrete. Water splash from impact. Urban alley
            background with graffiti. Dynamic, energetic. Commercial quality, 8K."
Assets:    --reference-images "product_urbanstep.png"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SHOT 2 — TRACKING (2s)
Context:   Runner moving through city
Subject:   Feet in motion, sneakers
Action:    Quick footwork, agility
Camera:    Low tracking, following feet
Prompt:    "Low-angle tracking shot following a runner's feet in black and
            neon green sneakers moving quickly across urban pavement.
            Dynamic motion, energy. Commercial sports style, 8K."
Assets:    --reference-images "product_urbanstep.png"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Context:   Full body, urban setting
Subject:   Athlete, confident stance
Action:   Steps forward, looks at camera
Camera:   Medium shot, static
Prompt:    "Medium shot of a young athlete in athletic wear stepping forward
            confidently in an urban setting. Black and neon green sneakers
            prominent. Direct eye contact with camera. Dynamic pose.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

            (UrbanStep Pro) floating in mid-air against a dark urban background.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Context:   Detail shot
Subject:   Sneaker sole pattern, neon green
Action:  Static, sharp focus
Camera:  ECU
Prompt:    "Extreme close-up of sneaker sole pattern, electric green rubber
            with geometric tread design. Macro photography, sharp detail,
            studio lighting. 8K."
Assets:    --reference-images "product_urbanstep.png"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```
