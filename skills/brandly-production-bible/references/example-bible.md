# Example: Lumière Perfume Campaign Bible

```markdown
# PRODUCTION BIBLE: Lumière — "First Light"
# Version: 1.2
# Created: 2026-01-15
# Last Updated: 2026-01-18

═══════════════════════════════════════

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

**Project Name:** Lumière — First Light
**Campaign Goal:** Launch Q2 perfume campaign, drive social engagement
**Target Audience:** Women 25-40, urban, aspirational, beauty-conscious
**Platform(s):** Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Pre-roll
**Total Budget (credits):** 200
**Target Duration:** 30s (primary), 15s cutdown (secondary)
**Deadline:** 2026-02-01
**Style Preset:** cinematic

───────────────────────────────────────

## 2. CREATIVE DIRECTION

### Mood
warm, intimate, aspirational, dreamy, luxurious

### Visual Tone
- Color direction: Golden hour palette — warm ambers, soft pinks, cream neutrals
- Texture direction: Soft skin, glass reflections, sheer fabrics, marble surfaces
- Light direction: Natural golden hour, soft diffusion, backlight for rim glow

### Audio Direction
- Music style: Ambient piano with subtle strings, building to emotional swell
- Voiceover: Yes — soft female voice, whisper-quiet intimacy
- SFX style: Minimal — subtle spray sound, fabric rustle

───────────────────────────────────────

## 3. BRAND STANDARDS

### Color Palette
| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Primary | #D4A574 | Warm gold, branding |
| Secondary | #F5E6D3 | Cream, backgrounds |
| Neutral | #2C2C2C | Text, dark accents |
| Accent | #C9A96E | Highlight, luxury detail |

### Typography
- Primary font: Playfair Display (headlines, elegance)
- Secondary font: Inter (body, CTAs)

### Logo Usage
- Placement: Bottom right in hero shot, 4s hold
- Size rules: Minimum 10% of frame height
- Variants: Gold foil (primary), white (dark backgrounds)

───────────────────────────────────────

## 4. CHARACTERS

### Character 1: Maya
- Sheet file: assets/characters/maya.png
- Key anchor traits: Olive skin (warm undertone), emerald green almond eyes,
  long dark brown hair in loose braids, subtle freckles across nose
- Must never change: Eye color, skin tone, hair color, braid style in this campaign
- Can vary: Expression, outfit (all outfits must be neutral/cream tones)
- Wardrobe variants: Cream silk slip dress (primary), white linen (optional B-roll)

───────────────────────────────────────

## 5. LOCATIONS

### Location 1: Modern Bedroom
- Sheet file: assets/locations/modern_bedroom.png
- Time of day: Golden hour (sunrise, ~6:30am)
- Key features: Floor-to-ceiling windows, white marble nightstand,
  rumpled white linens, sheer curtains, city skyline visible
- Consistency rule: Keep window light as primary source, never switch to artificial

───────────────────────────────────────

## 6. PRODUCTS

### Product: Lumière Perfume
- Sheet file: assets/products/lumiere.png
- Hero angles: Front 3/4 (primary), top-down (lifestyle), side profile (detail)
- Colors/variations: Clear glass bottle, rose gold cap, amber-gold liquid
- Logo placement: Etched on front of bottle, centered, small scale

───────────────────────────────────────

## 8. AUDIO PLAN

### Music
- Source: Generated (Agnes audio)
- BPM: 72 (slow, intimate)
- Mood: Dreamy piano, building strings

### Voiceover
- Script: "There is a moment... between sleep and waking... when the world feels new. This is that moment. Lumière."
- Voice type: Female, 30s, soft and intimate
- Timestamps: 0:04 (there is), 0:10 (between), 0:18 (this is), 0:25 (Lumière)

───────────────────────────────────────

## 9. EDITING PLAN

### Output Specs
- Format: MP4/H.264
- Resolution: 1080p (primary), 4K (archive)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (YouTube), 9:16 (social cutdown)
- Frame rate: 24fps (cinematic)

### Transition Style
Hard cuts, no transitions. Clean editorial.

### Color Grade
Warm lift in shadows, golden highlights, slight film grain overlay.
Reference: Kodak Vision3 500T look.
```
