# Pacing & Rhythm Guide

Pacing is the heartbeat of your campaign. Get it right and viewers feel the emotion. Get it wrong and the content falls flat.

## The Four Rhythm Types

### 1. Slow & Sweeping
**Best for:** Luxury, documentary, emotional storytelling
- Shot duration: 4–8 seconds
- Camera moves: slow pushes, gentle orbits
- Edit style: long holds, minimal cuts
- Music: ambient, sparse, building
- Use when: You want the viewer to breathe and absorb

### 2. Medium & Flowing
**Best for:** Commercial, lifestyle, product reveal
- Shot duration: 3–5 seconds
- Camera moves: steady tracking, moderate pushes
- Edit style: clean cuts on action
- Music: melodic, steady tempo
- Use when: You need clarity + emotional engagement

### 3. Fast & Energetic
**Best for:** Social ads, sports, youth brands
- Shot duration: 1–3 seconds
- Camera moves: whip pans, quick pushes, dynamic angles
- Edit style: rapid cuts, match cuts, rhythm edits
- Music: upbeat, percussive, driving beat
- Use when: You need to grab attention and convey energy

### 4. Staccato & Punchy
**Best for:** Tech launches, luxury reveals, dramatic moments
- Shot duration: 1–2 seconds for most, then hold on hero
- Camera moves: snap zooms, sudden stops
- Edit style: beat-synced cuts, silence for impact
- Music: minimal, then drop
- Use when: You need maximum impact per frame

## Beat-to-Shot Mapping

| Narrative Beat | Recommended Pacing | Shot Count | Duration Range |
|----------------|--------------------|------------|----------------|
| Hook/Attention | Fast | 2–3 | 1–2s each |
| Problem/Setup | Slow-Medium | 3–4 | 3–5s each |
| Reveal/Solution | Medium | 3–5 | 3–4s each |
| Emotional Peak | Slow | 2–3 | 4–6s each |
| Call to Action | Medium-Fast | 2–3 | 2–3s each |
| Logo/Outro | Slow | 1–2 | 3–5s each |

## The 3-Second Rule

AI video models maintain coherence best within 3–5 second clips.

**Rule:** No single shot should exceed 6 seconds unless it's an establishing aerial or a deliberate artistic choice.

Why? Beyond ~6s:
- Character faces begin to drift
- Lighting consistency degrades
- Product details shift
- Motion becomes unnatural

**Workaround for long actions:**
- Break into 3–4 shorter shots
- Use cutaways and reaction shots
- Repeat the action from different angles

## Rhythm Patterns

### The Crescendo (building energy)
```
Shot 1: EST, 4s — slow push in
Shot 2: MS, 3s — subject enters frame
Shot 3: CU, 2s — face, emotion
Shot 4: ECU, 2s — eye detail, climax
Shot 5: ORB, 4s — full reveal, hero moment
```
**Total:** 15s | **Pacing:** accelerates then releases

### The Breath (relaxed, luxurious)
```
Shot 1: AER, 6s — slow landscape sweep
Shot 2: W, 5s — subject in environment
Shot 3: TRACK, 4s — gentle follow
Shot 4: CU, 4s — intimate detail
Shot 5: PULL, 5s — pull back to reveal all
```
**Total:** 24s | **Pacing:** even, meditative

### The Pulse (rhythmic, syncopated)
```
Shot 1: CU, 1.5s — product detail snap
Shot 2: MS, 1.5s — subject interaction
Shot 3: ECU, 1s — texture/macro
Shot 4: W, 2s — full scene context
Shot 5: CU, 1.5s — emotion payoff
Shot 6: LOGO, 3s — hold on brand
```
**Total:** 10.5s | **Pacing:** staccato, beat-driven

## Emotional Pacing Cheat Sheet

| Emotion | Recommended Pacing | Camera Style |
|---------|-------------------|--------------|
| Excitement | Fast cuts, dynamic moves | Tracking, whip pans |
| Calm | Slow holds, gentle moves | Static, slow push |
| Mystery | Slow reveals, hidden details | Pull out, orbital |
| Romance | Medium-slow, soft focus | CU, OTS, gentle TRACK |
| Power | Slow buildup, then fast payoff | LA, ORB, PUSH |
| Nostalgia | Slow, warm, lingering | HA, static, warm grade |
| Urgency | Fast, sharp, abrupt | Quick cuts, ECU |
| Wonder | Slow, expansive, grand | AER, W, ORB |

## Tips

1. **Lead with the hook** — first 2 seconds determine if someone watches
2. **Vary your pace** — monotony kills engagement, mix slow and fast
3. **End strong** — final shot should be your hero/reveal/CTA
4. **Leave room to breathe** — not every frame needs action
5. **Match music to edit** — count beats, cut on rhythm
6. **Test with sound off** — if it still works visually, pacing is right
