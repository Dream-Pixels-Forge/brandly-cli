# Plant Species Reference

Common plants for botanical accuracy.

## Indoor Houseplants

### Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)
- Heart-shaped leaves
- Trailing/vining growth
- variegated green/yellow
- Low light tolerant

### Snake Plant (Sansevieria)
- Upright, sword-like leaves
- Green with yellow edges
- Very drought tolerant
- Air purifying

### Monstera (Monstera deliciosa)
- Large split leaves
- Tropical aesthetic
- Climbing habit
- Bright indirect light

### Fiddle Leaf Fig (Ficus lyrata)
- Large violin-shaped leaves
- Upright tree form
- Statement plant
- Bright filtered light

### Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum)
- Arching variegated leaves
- Produces baby plantlets
- Easy to propagate
- adaptable to conditions
