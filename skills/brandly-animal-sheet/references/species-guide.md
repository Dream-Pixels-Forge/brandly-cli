# Species Guide

Quick reference for common animal species in campaigns.

## Domestic Pets

### Dogs
**Popular breeds:**
- Golden Retriever: Friendly, loyal, family-oriented
- Labrador: Versatile, energetic, trainable
- French Bulldog: Compact, stylish, urban
- German Shepherd: Loyal, intelligent, protective
- Poodle: Elegant, hypoallergenic, versatile

**Visual notes:**
- Healthy, clean coat
- Engaging eyes
- Natural poses
- Breed-specific characteristics

### Cats
**Popular breeds:**
- Siamese: Elegant, vocal, blue eyes
- Persian: Fluffy, calm, luxurious
- Maine Coon: Large, friendly, majestic
- Scottish Fold: Unique ears, sweet expression
- Bengal: Wild-looking, spotted, energetic

**Visual notes:**
- Clean, glossy fur
- Expressive eyes
- Natural behaviors
- Breed traits visible

## Wildlife

### Large Mammals
- Lions: Pride dynamics, savanna setting
- Elephants: Family groups, dignity
- Wolves: Pack behavior, wild settings
- Bears: Natural habitat, power
- Deer: Graceful, forest settings

### Birds
- Eagles: Majestic, flight shots
- Penguins: Colony behavior, cold environments
- Parrots: Colorful, tropical settings
- Owls: Nocturnal, mystical
- Flamingos: Flocks, pink aesthetics

### Marine Life
- Dolphins: Playful, ocean settings
- Whales: Grand scale, oceanic
- Sea turtles: Graceful, underwater
- Coral reefs: Colorful, diverse

## Insects & Small Creatures

### Butterflies
- Monarch: Orange/black, migration
- Morpho: Iridescent blue, tropical
- Swallowtail: Elegant, garden settings

### Beetles
- Ladybug: Red/spotted, garden
- Rhinoceros beetle: Horned, tropical
- Stag beetle: Mandibles, forest

### Other Insects
- Bees: Pollination, hive context
- Fireflies: Bioluminescence, night
- Dragonflies: Metallic, near water

## Fantasy Creatures

### Dragon
**Elements:**
- Scales texture
- Wings spread
- Fire/elemental effects
- Epic lighting

### Unicorn
**Elements:**
- Flowing mane
- Spiral horn
- Ethereal glow
- Magical atmosphere

### Phoenix
**Elements:**
- Fire/ashes
- Rising motion
- Feather detail
- Dramatic lighting

## Naming Conventions

Always use scientific names for accuracy:
- `Canis lupus familiaris` (Dog)
- `Felis catus` (Cat)
- `Panthera leo` (Lion)
- `Equus ferus caballus` (Horse)

This helps with AI generation precision.
